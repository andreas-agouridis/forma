A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Forma is a browser-based AI image studio powered by your connection to Pollinations.

Need a hand? Read the guide ↗
Is Forma a free AI image generator?

Forma’s studio interface, prompt presets and downloads are free to use. AI generation and AI prompt refinement run through your Pollinations account. Provider credits, free-credit availability, model access and rate limits apply; unlimited free generation is not promised. Check your provider account ↗

How do I generate an image from text?

Connect Pollinations, describe your subject and setting, select a style and image model, then choose Generate image. When the result is ready, download the original image or reuse its prompt and settings.

Which AI model should I choose?

Z-Image Turbo is a fast starting point. FLUX.1 Schnell is suited to low-cost iteration, FLUX.2 Klein to creative detail, FLUX.2 Pro to high fidelity, and GPT Image 1 Mini to text and composition. Availability, provider costs and model access can change. Compare the models.

Are my prompts and images private?

Forma keeps drafts and recent creations in your browser when storage is available. Generation sends your prompt and settings directly to Pollinations. AI refinement also sends your prompt to Pollinations. Access tokens stay in page memory. Pollinations’ own data practices apply to requests it processes. Read how data is handled.

What happens if generation fails?

Forma retries temporary network, rate-limit and server failures up to two times using the same request and seed. Authentication, credit and permission errors show specific recovery steps. You can cancel a request and keep your prompt. Cancelling locally does not guarantee the provider stops processing or charging for work already started.
