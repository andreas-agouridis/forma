What the publishing kit includes

  • Your complete static studio, guide and privacy pages, with absolute canonical URLs and page-specific Open Graph and Twitter metadata in the original HTML.
  • Structured data with real page URLs, matching the visible application, guide and FAQ content.
  • A 1200 × 630 social-preview PNG, a favicon and an Apple touch icon, generated locally from Forma’s visual identity.
  • sitemap.xml containing the three public content pages, and robots.txt with the correct sitemap URL.
  • llms.txt and llms-full.txt with factual summaries, source links and readable site content for tools that support these emerging conventions.

Publish in three steps

  1. Enter your real URL, generate the ZIP, and unzip its contents into the corresponding static hosting folder.
  2. Place robots.txt at the domain root. If the app lives in a subdirectory, merge the generated sitemap line into an existing root robots.txt instead of replacing unrelated rules. Put the generated llms files at the root if you want the conventional discovery location.
  3. Open the hosted site, check canonical and social preview URLs, then submit the sitemap in Google Search Console ↗ and Bing Webmaster Tools ↗.

The original HTML already includes descriptive titles, useful content, accessible navigation and structured data. This helper adds the domain-specific information that cannot be finalized before a hosting address is known.

SEO, GEO and AI discoverability

GEO here means generative engine optimization: making the site’s actual features, instructions, limits and sources clear enough to retrieve and cite. It does not mean adding geographic targeting.

The public pages use direct answers, semantic headings, source citations, descriptive internal links, and matching structured data. The interactive studio and private local image history are separate from the crawlable guide and product explanations. No user prompts, images or tokens are included in this publishing package.

Crawler access and machine-readable summaries support discovery. Search indexing, rankings, rich results and AI citations are decisions made by the respective services, rather than features an application can guarantee.
